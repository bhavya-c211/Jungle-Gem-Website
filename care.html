Through the year

Christiansburg · zone 7a
Dec–Feb

Water less. Dry furnace air — add humidity. Keep leaves off cold glass.

Mar–May

Repot, start feeding. Plants go out after last frost, ~mid-May.

Jun–Aug

Water more. Shade from afternoon sun. Check for pests.

Sep–Nov

Bring tropicals in before first frost, ~mid-October.
